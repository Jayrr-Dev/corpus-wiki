# Brightwater Utilities: Confined Space Entry Procedure

Brightwater Utilities Ltd. is a fictional water utility. This procedure applies to every Brightwater field crew entering a vault, manhole or tank classed as a permit-required confined space.

## Before entry

Every entry needs an Entry Supervisor, an Attendant who stays outside the space for the whole entry, and at least one Entrant. One person may not hold two of these roles during the same entry.

## Entry steps

Crews follow these steps in this order. Do not skip or reorder steps.

1. The Entry Supervisor opens a confined space entry permit in the Brightwater Field App and records the space ID, the crew and the expected duration.
2. The crew isolates every energy source that feeds the space, including pumps, valves and electrical feeds, and applies a personal lock and tag to each isolation point.
3. The crew ventilates the space with a forced-air blower for at least 15 minutes before testing the atmosphere.
4. The Attendant tests the atmosphere at the top, middle and bottom of the space with a calibrated four-gas monitor.
5. The Entry Supervisor confirms that oxygen is between 19.5% and 23.5%, flammable gas is below 10% of the lower explosive limit, carbon monoxide is below 25 ppm and hydrogen sulfide is below 10 ppm, and records the readings on the permit.
6. The Entry Supervisor signs the permit, which authorizes entry for at most 8 hours.
7. Each Entrant puts on the required equipment and is attached to the retrieval system before entering.
8. The Attendant keeps continuous contact with every Entrant and keeps the four-gas monitor running for the whole entry.
9. If any reading leaves the safe range, the Attendant orders everyone out at once and the permit is suspended until the space is tested again.
10. When the work is done, every Entrant leaves the space and the Attendant counts them out.
11. The crew removes its locks and tags and restores the energy sources.
12. The Entry Supervisor closes the permit in the Brightwater Field App within 24 hours of the entry.

## Required equipment

Every Entrant must wear all of the following, and nothing on this list is optional:

- a full-body harness
- a hard hat
- a personal four-gas monitor
- a two-way radio

## Who may act as Attendant

Any one of the following people may act as Attendant:

- a crew member who completed Brightwater's confined space course within the last 2 years
- a Brightwater safety officer
- a contractor who holds a current confined space rescue certificate

## Records

Brightwater keeps every closed confined space entry permit for 5 years.
