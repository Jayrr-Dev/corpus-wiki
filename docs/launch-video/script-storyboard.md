# Launch video — script + storyboard (≈2:00, 16:9 horizontal)

Modelled on the reference reel (Video-91580.mp4). Here's how that reel works:

- **Format:** the reference is 720×1280 vertical. **This video is 16:9 horizontal, 1920×1080**, with the same warm natural-light film look and shallow depth of field.
- **Rhythm:** a cut every 1.5–3 s. Talking head → screen recording → talking head.
- **Captions:** 2–3 words at a time, white, centre of frame, soft shadow.
- **Screen B-roll:** real app footage, zoomed in tight, with a big cursor and slow push-ins.
- **Structure:** hook → who I am → the problem → "so I built it" → fast demo montage → social proof → relaxed CTA. It ends on a casual location (lying on the floor) to feel human.

**Going horizontal:** frame talking heads off-centre (rule of thirds) with the window or room visible on the other side. Screen recordings can show more of the app at once, so zoom less and push in slowly. Captions sit in the lower third, not the centre.

**Shot types** (in the TYPE column):
- **CAM**: filmed talking head.
- **SCR**: real screen recording of the app.
- **HF**: motion graphic you can build later in HyperFrames (HTML → video). The agent writes it; you tweak and render.
- **INS**: filmed insert (hands, keyboard, office).

Target: ~300 spoken words at ~150 wpm.
