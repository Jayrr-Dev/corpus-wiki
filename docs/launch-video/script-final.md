## Act 2: The company problem

```text
[Casual, talking to a friend] I've worked at a bunch of private companies. And the hardest thing for all of them wasn't sales, or hiring... [leaning in, sincere] it was writing down what people KNOW.

[Remembering, slightly amused] At one place, our most senior engineer left on a Friday. [short pause] By Monday, nobody knew how half the system worked. [dry laugh] We spent WEEKS piecing it back together from old emails and chat messages.

[Quieter, a little deflated] Someone leaves, and years of context are just... gone.

[Wry, slightly exasperated] And the docs that DO exist? Stale. Nobody trusts them. [sighs] So people just ask each other. Over... and over.
```

## Act 3: My own pain

```text
[Warmer, storytelling] So I tried fixing it for myself first. Obsidian, Notion, all of them. [genuinely impressed] And honestly? The graphs are gorgeous.

[Tired, half-laughing] But making them is SO tedious. Every note, you're linking things by hand. [dry chuckle] It turns into a second job.

[Softer, wistful] I always wished it would just... do that for me.

[long pause]
```

## Act 4: The reveal and demo

```text
[Brightening, quietly proud] So I built it. [confident] It's called Corpus Wiki.

[Energetic, quicker pace] You just feed it. PDFs, Word docs, notes, screenshots... even videos. Or connect your Gmail.

[Clear, confident] It pulls out the facts, and writes the wiki for you. Every line... linked to where it came from.

[Building excitement] And it connects everything. The graph, the glossary, who's who... AUTOMATICALLY.

[Playful, grinning] No brackets. Ever.

[Curious, demonstrating] Ask it anything... like, "why did we switch payment providers?" [short pause] And it answers from what your team actually wrote. With sources.

[Matter-of-fact] Drop in a newer file, and it only rewrites the lines that changed. [short pause] And if two files disagree... it asks you first.

[Thoughtful] A normal wiki only knows what someone remembered to write down. [short pause] This one keeps up on its own.
```

## Hackathon section: Nemotron, Token Factory and Nebius cloud

```text
[Shifting gear, clear and confident] So how does it actually work?

[Explaining, steady] Every AI step runs on open NVIDIA Nemotron models... served by Nebius Token Factory.

[Walking through it] Nemotron Super does the everyday work. It reads each upload, writes the lines, and fact-checks them. Nemotron Ultra makes the big calls, like planning articles and building the topic tree. Nano handles the bulk linking. And the vision model reads your screenshots, diagrams and video frames.

[Matter-of-fact, a little proud] Token Factory gives us one API for all of them, with structured output, so every change comes back valid. Each step gets the cheapest model that does its job well... and I can switch any of them in settings.
```
