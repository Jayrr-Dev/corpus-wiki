Billing sync: how it works

Owner: Dave  ·  14 views this year

Overview

Invoices sync from the billing provider every night at 02:00. Failed runs retry three times, then page the on-call engineer.

Running it by hand

Use sync-billing --full from the old ops box. Ask Dave for the VPN key before you start.

Known issues

Refunds over 90 days are skipped. A fix is planned for next quarter.
