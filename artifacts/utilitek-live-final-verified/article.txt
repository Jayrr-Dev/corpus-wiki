Distribution system design

Distribution system design at Utilitek Solutions covers overhead and underground distribution networks. The firm describes work for urban, suburban, and rural settings, including pole-load analysis, anchors, framing, guyed structures, and conductor sizing. Shared-pole engineering is listed among its specialisms.[4][5][2]Updated Oct 17, 2026

Residential development support

Residential development support at Utilitek Solutions covers the electrical work for housing developments. Its coordination includes feeder backbones, pad-mounted transformers, lighting, and individual lot services, along with permits and developer cost estimates.[2]Updated Oct 17, 2026

Utility power systems

Utility power systems work at Utilitek Solutions covers substations, protection and control, and three-phase and single-phase distribution. The offering also lists transformer installations and service laterals.[2][1]Updated Oct 17, 2026

Protection and control work involves relays, CT/PT equipment, SCADA, fault detection, and breaker operation.[4]Updated Oct 17, 2026
